OPNsense) and all the rest of our work.

## Become a sponsor

1. Open [github.com/sponsors/swissmakers](https://github.com/sponsors/swissmakers) and pick a tier.
2. Company sponsors: send us your logo (SVG or PNG, at least 120 px wide) and the link
   you want it to point to, either through the GitHub Sponsors message or by
   [opening an issue](https://github.com/swissmakers/fail2ban-ui/issues/new).
3. We add your logo to this file, the README and fail2ban-ui.com with the next release.

Sponsorship does not buy priority for feature requests or influence over the roadmap.

Not in a position to sponsor? Reporting a reproducible bug, improving the docs or
[translating the UI](CONTRIBUTING.md) helps just as much.
